The console

GTA 6 isn’t on PS4 or Xbox One. On an Xbox Series S, the game takes more than half of the usable space: plan for extra storage.

Storage

For a PS5 SSD, check these points before you buy:

  • An M.2 NVMe SSD on PCIe Gen4 x4 (Gen3 won’t work).
  • At least 5,500 MB/s sequential read speed, as recommended by Sony.
  • Form factor 2230, 2242, 2260, 2280 or 22110: 2280 is the most common.
  • A heatsink, built in or added, that fits Sony’s size limits.

On Xbox Series, only a storage expansion card can run games: a USB drive can only store them.
