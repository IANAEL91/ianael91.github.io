Free up space on PS5

  • Delete games you no longer play: they stay in your library and your saves stay on the console.
  • Move PS5 games to a USB drive: it stores them, but you’ll need to move them back to play.
  • Add an M.2 SSD (NVMe PCIe 4.0, with a heatsink) to the PS5 expansion slot: games run straight from it.

Free up space on Xbox

  • Uninstall games you no longer play: they stay in your collection.
  • A USB drive can store Xbox Series games, but it can’t run them.
  • Add a storage expansion card for Xbox Series: games run from it just like from the internal SSD.

See Xbox expansion cards

Where these numbers come from

Rockstar says the game needs up to 212 GB depending on the device. The usable space for each console is an average reported by the press.
