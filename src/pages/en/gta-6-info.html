Release, price, storage, length, trophies, online and PC. Every answer links to its source. Updated November 19, 2026.

When did GTA 6 come out?

On Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. On PlayStation, it unlocked at midnight local time, starting with New Zealand.

Sources (in French): Xboxygen, Selectra

How long does it take to beat GTA 6?

About 80 hours for the story plus some activities: that’s how long Rockstar says a full playthrough can take. Rushing the story gets you to the credits sooner. 100% and the platinum take much longer.

Source: TechRadar

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In the US, $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. Black Friday deals run until Cyber Monday, November 30: compare before you buy.

How many GB is GTA 6?

Up to 212 GB depending on your console, according to Rockstar. On a standard PS5, with about 667 GB usable, that’s almost a third of the space. On an Xbox Series S, with about 364 GB, it’s more than half.

How many trophies does GTA 6 have?

55: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. 28 are hidden, mostly tied to the story. None require playing online, so the platinum is fully single-player.

Which consoles is GTA 6 on?

Only PS5, including PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S. There’s no PS4 or Xbox One version.

Source: Techloy

Is the online mode available?

No, GTA 6 launched as a single-player game. Rockstar hasn’t announced a date or details for an online mode. Twitch’s CEO hinted at 2027, without confirmation from Rockstar.

Sources: DLCompare, GTABase

Will GTA 6 come to PC?

No PC version has been announced. Take-Two’s CEO says Rockstar is focusing on console players first. If the studio follows its habits, a PC version would rather arrive in late 2027 or 2028: it took about 19 months for GTA 5 and 12 months for Red Dead Redemption 2.

Sources: Tbreak, AllThings.how

Does the boxed version include a disc?

No: the box contains a download code. If your code is missing or unreadable, keep your receipt and contact the store or PlayStation support.
